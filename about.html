About TK Company

TK Company is a creative studio founded by Redin and Darios, built on storytelling, design, and innovation. From books and animations to games and visual languages, we craft worlds that feel alive.

Our mission is to turn imagination into reality — one character, one symbol, one idea at a time. Whether it's the bold adventures of TG and Kai or the harmful chaos of Yatkar Man, every project is a piece of our vision.

We believe in friendship, humor, and the power of originality. TK is more than a company — it's a movement.
